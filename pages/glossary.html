Mahjong Glossary

A quick reference to vocabulary used throughout this website.

TermMeaning
ChiClaim a tile to complete a sequence, normally from the previous player.
PongClaim a matching discard to form a triplet.
KongFour identical tiles treated as a single meld, normally with a replacement draw.
Chow / SequenceThree consecutive numbered tiles in one suit.
Pung / TripletThree identical tiles.
PairTwo identical tiles.
Winning handA hand that meets the table's winning conditions.
Self-drawWin using a tile drawn by yourself.
DealerPlayer who starts the hand and usually discards first.
Flower tileA bonus tile typically set aside and replaced.
TaiA unit in many Taiwanese scoring systems; values vary by table.
Similar terms in different mahjong variants do not always mean identical rules.
