Tile Actions: Chi, Pong, and Kong

Three common ways to create melds using numbered or identical tiles.

Chi — A sequence

Holding 2 Character and 3 Character, you can normally claim 1 Character discarded by the immediately preceding player to form a sequence.

1 Character2 Character3 Character

Chi normally takes a discard only from the previous player.

Pong — A triplet

Holding two East tiles, you can normally claim a matching East discard to form a triplet.

EastEastEast

Kong — Four identical tiles

Four identical tiles can form a Kong, which counts as one meld and typically requires a replacement draw.

House rules: Claim priority and certain Kong restrictions vary. Confirm your table's practices.
