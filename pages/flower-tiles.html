Flower Tiles and Replacement Draws

In many Taiwanese 16-tile mahjong games, eight bonus tiles represent four flowers and four seasons. They do not count toward the structural 16 tiles in your resting hand.

Remember: Display a bonus tile and take a replacement tile according to your table's rules.

The eight bonus tiles

A typical Taiwanese mahjong set has four flower tiles and four season tiles.

Four flowers
PlumOrchidChrysanthemumBamboo
Four seasons
SpringSummerAutumnWinter

What happens when you draw a flower?

Typically, you reveal the bonus tile, put it aside face up, and draw a replacement tile. If the replacement is another bonus tile, continue according to your table's replacement procedure. The bonus tile is not used to form a sequence, triplet, Kong or pair.

Example

You draw a Spring bonus tile while holding 16 structural tiles.

Step 1: Reveal Spring and place it in your bonus area.

Step 2: Draw a replacement tile from the table's designated replacement end of the wall.

Step 3: If the replacement is a regular tile, continue the turn as permitted by the rules.

How do flowers affect scoring?

Flower and season tiles may contribute scoring units called Tai. Some rules award bonuses for matching your seat, gathering groups of flowers, or collecting all eight. The exact combinations, point values, and special winning conditions vary between rule sets.

House-rule warning: Before playing, agree on flower scoring and replacement-draw procedures. Do not assume one published scoring table applies everywhere.
