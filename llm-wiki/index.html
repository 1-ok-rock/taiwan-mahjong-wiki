Standard hand

A resting hand has 16 structural tiles. The dealer starts with 17 before the first discard.

Wiki evidence: S01, S02, S03, S05

Winning hand

A normal winning hand has five sets and a pair (5×3+2=17 structural tiles). A declared kong adds a physical tile.

Wiki evidence: S01, S02, S03, S05

Tile families

Characters, Dots, Bamboo, Winds, Dragons, and eight bonus Flowers/Seasons; normally 144 physical tiles.

Wiki evidence: S02, S03, S05

Chow / Chi

A set of three consecutive numbered tiles in one suit, ordinarily claimed from the previous player.

Wiki evidence: S01, S04, S05

Pung / Pong

Three identical tiles; a discard may be claimed to complete the set.

Wiki evidence: S01, S04, S05

Kong

Four identical tiles count as one set; a replacement draw normally follows.

Wiki evidence: S02, S04, S05

Flower

A Flower/Season is exposed and replaced; it does not form a normal chow, pung, or pair.

Wiki evidence: S02, S04, S05

Scoring differences

Tai, the minimum winning requirement and payments vary between rulesets: do not treat one platform's scoring table as universal.

Wiki evidence: S01, S02, S03, S05
