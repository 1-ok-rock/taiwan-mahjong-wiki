## Curated study notes
Four players use a 144-tile set. Nondealer players start with sixteen tiles while East begins with seventeen and discards. A normal winning hand consists of five sets plus one pair. Flowers and seasons are exposed and replaced. This online ruleset does not require minimum tai; its scoring settings are not universal.
