## Curated study notes
This particular Taiwanese Classic preset teaches a sixteen-tile hand and a seventeen-tile normal win made of five sets and a pair. The set has 144 tiles including bonus flowers. It permits a zero-tai win and adds a base stake plus tai in its payment formula. Local tables can vary.
