## Curated study notes
A beginner introduction covers four players, 144 tiles, three numbered suits and honor tiles. Dealer starts with seventeen. During play, players draw and discard; a chow is a three-tile sequence and a pung is a matching triplet. Flower replacement and scoring conventions vary among sources.
