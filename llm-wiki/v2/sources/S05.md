## Curated study notes
The Taiwanese rules implementation describes sixteen structural tiles while waiting and seventeen on a regular win of five melds plus a pair, normally with 144 physical tiles. It states that specific kong-claim restrictions and its scoring values are local site rules and should not be universalized.
