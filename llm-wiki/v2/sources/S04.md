## Curated study notes
A turn is a draw followed by a discard except when a call or win intervenes. Chows require three consecutive suited tiles and are taken from the preceding player. Pungs have three matching tiles. A declared kong uses four matching tiles and requires a replacement draw. Flowers also require replacement.
